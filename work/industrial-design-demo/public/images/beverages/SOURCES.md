## Models
Downloaded `sprite.glb`, `coca.glb`, `fanta.glb` from https://github.com/rahoolkumardev/Coke-3d-Model/tree/main . No LICENSE file was found at repository root. These are third-party brand models for the local prototype; public redistribution/commercial permission has not been verified. Packaging shown by these assets may differ from the Chinese-market product and from task volume. No remote deployment was performed.

Jasmine tea lead: https://www.baoxiaohe.com/models/3092/ (online packaging editor; no directly downloadable GLB obtained).
Orange pulp drink lead: https://m.mfcad.com/tuzhi/show_196620.html (Pro/E bottle CAD, listed at 5 platform credits, not a textured GLB). No purchase made.
Both remain explicitly unconfigured in product resources; no generic object is presented as the correct branded model.
