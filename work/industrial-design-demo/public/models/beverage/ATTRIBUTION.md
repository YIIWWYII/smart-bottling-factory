## Yibao Holiday Moment peach beverage

- yibao-peach-bottle.glb: model rebuilt locally in Blender from the three product photographs supplied by the user on 2026-11-12. White cap, tall PET bottle, ivory-to-pink wrap. Dimensions are approximate display proportions, not physical measurements.
- yibao-peach-label.png: cylindrical unwrap from the user's front-label photograph. The unobserved rear uses a plain colour continuation; it is not a complete official packaging file.
- Editable source: blender-integration/beverage/yibao-peach-bottle.blend and build_yibao_peach.py, relative to the web project.
- images/beverages/yibao-peach.webp: peach, blossom and water background supplied by the user; retained as provided apart from WebP encoding.
- images/beverages/cola-lovart.webp: background generated using Lovart (Seedream), project https://www.lovart.ai/canvas?projectId=2vpm4sdcgrnk .
